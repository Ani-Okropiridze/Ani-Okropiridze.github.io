Fixed Income (Rutgers Business School)

Ford Motor Company – Credit Worthiness Report

A fixed income analysis of Ford Motor Company from a bond investor's perspective, built to decide whether to invest in Ford's debt. It covers the income statement, balance sheet and cash flows, liquidity, leverage (debt/EBITDA), fixed charge coverage, the debt maturity profile, management, and key risks such as tariffs, EV losses and a possible rating downgrade. Conclusion: invest in Ford's debt, supported by its BBB- investment-grade rating, a 4.7x fixed charge coverage and a strong U.S. franchise.

Southwest Airlines vs. United Airlines – Equity Analysis

A five-person team presentation comparing Southwest (LUV) and United (UAL) on revenue, profitability, valuation, capital structure, fleet strategy and the 2026 jet fuel crisis, with bull and bear cases for each. Conclusion: United is the stronger business today, while Southwest is the higher-conviction turnaround bet.
